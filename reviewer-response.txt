SCOPE CORRECTIONS (please read first)
-------------------------------------
Two items in the original D1 commitment no longer describe what we built. We are
flagging this ourselves rather than letting a re-review discover it.

1. Privy is not integrated, and will not be. We moved to a non-custodial model:
   contributors hold their own keys in Freighter / Lobstr / xBull / Albedo and
   prove account ownership by signing a SEP-10 challenge. There is therefore no
   Privy-provisioned contributor account to show. Delegated transaction
   construction still exists (the client describes intent, the backend builds and
   submits), but the signer is the user's own wallet, not a Privy embedded wallet.

2. No HSM/KMS is in service, because the non-custodial pivot removed what it was
   for. We no longer hold contributor keys at all. The only long-lived secrets
   PathPulse holds are two service keys: the SDP distribution account, and the
   SEP-10 server signing key. On testnet both are environment-held. The Signer
   interface (backend/src/stellar/signing.ts) abstracts the backend, and the dev
   signer refuses to produce a mainnet signature outright; the aws-kms / gcp-kms /
   hsm branches are declared but not implemented. We would rather state this
   plainly than describe a KMS setup that does not exist.

D2 - DEMO URL AND EXTERNAL WALLET TRANSACTION
----------------------------------------------
Demo URL:  https://demo.pathpulse.ai
Test page: https://demo.pathpulse.ai/dashboard/profile
Backend:   https://demo-api.pathpulse.ai

Before you start: set your wallet to the Stellar TESTNET network. The demo
builds and submits testnet transactions only, and a wallet left on mainnet will
fail to sign.

Reviewer path (please follow in this order):
  1. Go to https://demo.pathpulse.ai/signin and choose "Connect Wallet".
     Your wallet signs a SEP-10 challenge; this establishes the session.
  2. Go to https://demo.pathpulse.ai/dashboard/profile
     The wallet panel is shown there, with your account balance read live
     from Horizon.
  3. If the panel reports "Account not found on testnet", click
     "Fund with Friendbot". You do not need XLM from us.
  4. Click "Reconnect wallet to send a payment". This is expected - the
     signing connection is not carried across the page navigation, so the
     wallet is re-attached for signing.
  5. Leave the destination field empty (it defaults to your own account) or
     paste any testnet address, then click "Send 1 XLM".
  6. Your wallet prompts for a signature. The signed envelope is submitted
     directly to Horizon and the resulting hash is displayed in the panel,
     with a stellar.expert testnet link next to it.

Freighter is the wallet we have exercised end to end. Lobstr's SEP-10 flow
requires a stellar.toml at the home domain, which we are adding - if you are
testing today, please use Freighter, xBull or Albedo.

The payment is built client-side, signed inside your own wallet extension, and
submitted directly to Horizon - the backend never holds a key and is not in the
signing path. The resulting hash is displayed in the panel.

Open endpoints for inspection:
  /health                 service status and network
  /v1/treasury/config     treasury signer set, read live from Horizon

KNOWN GAPS, D2:
  (a) An already-signed external-wallet transaction hash is not included in this
      message. It will follow.
  (b) None. Google sign-in and SEP-10 wallet sign-in both persist a session on
      this host.

KNOWN GAP, D3:
  The driver-pool weightings in these four batches (1.0x / 1.2x / 1.5x) were taken
  from the settlement request, not from on-chain SCOUT badges. The recipient
  accounts hold no SCOUT trustline. The engine reads the chain first and falls back
  to the supplied tier when no badge is held (backend/src/stellar/settlement.ts:110)
  and in these runs it fell back. The split arithmetic is exact and verifiable; the
  reputation input to it was not chain-sourced in these particular batches. We are
  re-running a batch with badges actually issued so the multiplier is verifiable
  end to end.
